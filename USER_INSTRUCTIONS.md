## UI/UX & Design Philosophy (The Master Standards)
[Card-Based Layout & Hierarchy]:

Independent Units: Group functions into independent 'Cards' with generous padding and clear visual separation.

Visual Hierarchy: Place critical info at the top with larger sizes; secondary menus go below in list format.

Negative Space: Maintain wide intervals between elements to ensure visual comfort and reduce cognitive load.

[Shape, Depth & Roundness]:

Uniform Roundness: Apply a consistent, large curvature (16px to 24px radius) to all cards, buttons, and inputs for a friendly, modern look.

Subtle Elevation: Use multi-layered shadows and thin borders to create a 3D "tangible" feel and clear layer distinction.

[High-End Typography & Icons]:

Weight-Based Hierarchy: Distinguish headers from body text primarily through Font Weight and Size, rather than just color.

Symbolic Iconography: Use intuitive, clean icons (Lucide, etc.) that represent functions instantly.

[Motion & Interaction]: Implement fluid animations (Framer Motion/GSAP) for scroll-linked effects and micro-interactions on clickable elements.

## Work Process (Mandatory)
[Blueprint First]: Present a detailed Work Blueprint (Structure, UI Flow, 3D/UI Concept) in Korean and receive user approval before coding.

[Target-Fixed Design]:

Admin/Back-office: PC-only layout (Grid-heavy, Naver Admin style).

Customer-facing: Mobile-only layout (Vertical, Toss/Kakao style).

[Sequential Delivery]: Break complex tasks into phases and confirm after each milestone.
